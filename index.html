Pioneer Academics

Biomedical research on
cancer & diabetes

Selected for Pioneer Academics — a highly competitive, college-level research program — to study disabling diseases including cancer and diabetes. Worked within a team of five to investigate biomedical engineering solutions with a focus on equity and access.

The program challenged me to think rigorously about how cutting-edge science translates into real interventions for communities that have historically been left behind by medical innovation.

Program

New York (Remote)
Period

Key findings & contributions
01
Cancer & diabetes systems study
Completed in-depth study of biological and systemic mechanisms behind cancer and diabetes, building a foundation in disease pathology and the engineering challenges of treatment design.
02
Low-cost medical device proposals
Collaborated with a team of five to design and propose low-cost medical device improvements for chronic disease management — prioritizing affordability and accessibility for underserved communities.
03
Equity-centered intervention design
Framed all proposals around access — who benefits from existing medical technology, and how engineering design choices can expand or restrict that access.
"The communities most in need of medical innovation are often the least represented in the research that produces it."
